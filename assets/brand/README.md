# Brand assets

Put the **official** GDG logo files for the chapter here (for example `gdg-logo.png` or `.svg`), then uncomment the logo line at the top of the main `README.md`.

## Where to get them
GDG chapters receive official branding assets and guidelines through the GDG program. Ask the chapter organisers (see [`docs/community-aast-abu-qir.md`](../../docs/community-aast-abu-qir.md)) for the current logo files and the brand guidelines. Don't download logos from random websites, since versions get outdated.

## Brand rules (the short version)
- Use the **official files** only. Do not redraw, recolour, stretch, crop, rotate or animate the logo.
- Keep clear space around it and don't place it on busy backgrounds.
- Use the correct chapter name: **GDG on Campus AASTMT Abu Qir** (the platform lists it as "GDG on Campus Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport - Abu Qir, Egypt").
- Don't imply that Google endorses this project. Keep the disclaimer below on public pages.
- Check the guidelines before putting the logo on merch, slides or social posts.

## Disclaimer to keep on public pages
> Community content for GDG on Campus AASTMT Abu Qir. This is not an official Google product, and the views and activities here are not those of Google.
