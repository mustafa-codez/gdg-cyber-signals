# Our Chapter: GDG on Campus AAST Abu Qir

**GDG on Campus, Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT), Abu Qir, Egypt.**
The chapter describes itself as a volunteer-run community for students, graduates and tech enthusiasts: hackathons, workshops, tech talks and events.

## Tagging and posting
When you share a project from this repo (see [`../SHOWCASE.md`](../SHOWCASE.md)):
- Tag the chapter's LinkedIn page and Instagram account.
- Use `#GDG #GoogleDeveloperGroups #BuildWithAI`, and the chapter's own hashtags if it has them.
- Ask the chapter's media team about their post guidelines and whether they can reshare.
- Follow the official GDG brand rules for logos and names.

## Student perks worth knowing
At its info session the chapter presented a student offer for Gemini. Availability, plan and deadlines vary by country and change, so check the official page: https://gemini.google/students/ (and https://one.google.com/ai-student).
