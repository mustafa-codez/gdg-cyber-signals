# Ethics and Safety Rules

Everything in this repository is for **learning and defence**. By using it you agree to the rules below.

## The golden rules

1. **Authorisation first.** Only test, scan, attack or probe systems you own or have *written* permission to test. Your own VMs and the practice platforms in `RESOURCES.md` are fine. Everything else is not.
2. **Isolation.** Run labs in VMs, containers or isolated networks. Never put a honeypot, vulnerable app or malware sample on a network you don't control.
3. **No real credentials, no real victims.** Use synthetic or public datasets. Never reuse real passwords in a lab.
4. **No malware you didn't write for the lab.** Labs here do not ask you to run live malware.

## OSINT and investigation rules (Scout, Ledger)

- Public information only. No breaking into accounts, no social engineering real people, no impersonation.
- **No doxxing.** Do not collect or publish private individuals' home addresses, family details, or anything that could cause real-world harm. Practise on public figures' *public roles*, organisations, or the provided exercises.
- **Proportionality and purpose.** Write down why you are collecting something before you collect it. If you cannot justify it, don't.
- Verify before you share. Wrongly accusing someone is a harm.
- Respect platform terms of service and local law, including data protection law.
- Tracing crypto or investigating scams: report findings to the platform or the relevant authority. Do not take "justice" into your own hands.

## LLM security rules (Aegis)

- Attack only the practice targets we provide or models and apps you own.
- Don't use jailbreak techniques to produce harmful content for real use. We study them to build defences.
- Do not test other people's production AI apps without permission or a published bug bounty or disclosure policy.

## Bio rules (Helix)

- Use only public, de-identified, or synthetic data.
- Genomic and neural data are among the most sensitive data that exist. Never upload real personal samples.
- No wet-lab or pathogen enhancement content. The track is about computation and data security only.

## Responsible disclosure

If you find a real vulnerability while learning, stop, do not exploit it, and report it through the organisation's disclosure policy (look for `security.txt` or a bug-bounty page).

## Breaking the rules

Violating these rules means removal from sessions and the repository. If unsure whether something is OK, ask first.
