# Code of Conduct

This project supports a Google Developer Groups (GDG) community. We follow the GDG community guidelines and the principles below.

## Our standard
- Be respectful, patient and welcoming. Beginners are the point of this repo.
- Assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome contact.
- Give credit. Don't present others' work as yours.
- Follow `ETHICS.md`. Security knowledge is used to defend, never to harm.

## Reporting
Report problems privately to the chapter organisers (add contact email here). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement
Organisers may warn, remove content, or remove participants from sessions and the repository.
